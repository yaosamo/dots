Dots & Clouds

Hello, we’re dots: small and fun everyday tools for your Mac

Click a dot to see what it does

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

For macOS 14 Sonoma or later

  • Selfie camera

    ⌃⇧1

    A floating bubble of your camera for calls and recordings. Circle, portrait or a wobbling blob, three sizes, and electric, fire, rainbow or cloud effects.

  • Tasks

    ⌃⇧2

    A list over everything. Add a task, check it off, drag to reorder, and it’s there next time.

  • Draw on screen

    ⌃⇧3

    Draw over any app, spotlight what matters, or flip to a whiteboard with shapes, arrows and text.

  • Timer

    ⌃⇧4

    A 3D die that bounces around your screen and counts down. It chimes and hops when time’s up.

  • Clipboard

    ⌃⇧5

    Your last five copies, text or images, to copy again with a click or a number key.

Pick only the dots you want. They wait at the top of your screen, and everything they hold stays on your Mac: no account, no tracking, nothing collected.
