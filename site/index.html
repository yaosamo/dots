Dots & Clouds

Hello, we’re dots – small and fun everyday tools for your computer

For macOS 14 Sonoma or later

  • Blob camera ⌃⇧1

    A floating camera bubble for calls and recordings. Resize it, reshape it, drag it anywhere.

  • Tasks ⌃⇧2

    A quick list over everything, with subtasks. Jot a task, check it off, get back to work.

  • Draw on screen ⌃⇧3

    Draw on any screen, spotlight what matters, or sketch on a whiteboard.

  • Timer ⌃⇧4

    A little die that tumbles to the bottom of your screen and counts down.

  • Clipboard ⌃⇧5

    Your recent copies, text or images, one click away from being copied again.
