Changelog

New updates and improvements to Dots & Clouds.

October 20261.1

Screenshots, framed

A new dot. Press ⌃⇧6, pick an area or a window, and it opens in a frame: a backdrop, a macOS or Safari title bar, arrows and boxes. Copy it or save it.

Improvements

  • Subtasks: drag a task a little to the right to tuck it under another. Tab and ⇧Tab work too.
  • The camera works with any camera, USB, virtual or your iPhone, and with a microphone: its edge glows as you speak.
  • Hide Dots in the menu bar menu keeps the bar off the screen. Shortcuts still work.
September 20261.0

Hello, dots

Five small tools at the top of your screen: a blob camera, tasks, a pen, a dice timer and clipboard history.
