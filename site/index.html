Dots & Clouds

Hello, we’re dots: small and fun everyday tools for your Mac

Click a dot to see what it does

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

For macOS 14 Sonoma or later

See them in action

  • Blob camera

    ⌃⇧1

    A floating bubble of your camera for calls and recordings. Circle, portrait or a wobbling blob, in three sizes. Dots Pro adds electric, fire, rainbow and cloud effects.

  • Tasks

    ⌃⇧2

    A list over everything. Type a task and press Return, check it off, drag to reorder. It’s still there next time.

  • Subtasks New in 1.1

    Tab

    Drag a task a little to the right under another and it becomes a subtask. Drag it back out to the left. Fold a task’s subtasks into a stack with ←, unfold with →.

  • Draw on screen

    ⌃⇧3

    Draw over any app with a red pen, or with Dots Pro’s electric, fire and rainbow brushes. Ink stays until you clear it.

  • Spotlight

    5

    Lasso what matters and everything else dims. Overlapping spotlights merge.

  • Whiteboard

    W

    Flip the pen to a whiteboard with shapes, arrows and text that snap to a dot grid. It’s saved for next time. Part of Dots Pro.

  • Timer

    ⌃⇧4

    A 3D die that drops in, bounces off the edges of your screen and counts down. Click to start, toss it around, and it chimes and hops when time’s up.

  • Clipboard

    ⌃⇧5

    Your recent copies, text or images, to copy again with a click or a number key. The last three, or the last ten with Dots Pro.

Pick only the dots you want. They wait at the top of your screen, or hide them and use just the shortcuts. Everything they hold stays on your Mac: no account, no tracking, nothing collected.
